The easiest Christmas gifts are things people use every day but rarely buy for themselves: a fragrance they love, a makeup or skincare treat, a grooming set or a better toothbrush. Here’s what our Pacific Fair team suggests, sorted by who you’re buying for, plus a few ways to make the shopping quicker.

## For the fragrance lover

Fragrance is a classic Christmas gift, and it’s easier to get right with a little help. Start with what they wear now (a photo of the bottle on their dresser is perfect), then ask our beauty advisors for a few similar scents to try on the testers.

Our [perfume and fragrance gifts page](/fragrance/) lists the designer and celebrity brands in our cabinets, and our [fragrance gift guide](/fragrance-gift-ideas/) explains scent families and the difference between EDT and EDP. Gift sets with a full-size bottle and a matching body lotion or travel spray usually arrive before Christmas, and they come ready to wrap.

## For the makeup lover

Unless you know their exact shade, choose something that doesn’t depend on it: an eyeshadow palette, a lip set, mascara, a setting spray or a good brush set. Our makeup brands include Maybelline, L’Oréal Paris, Revlon, Rimmel London, Max Factor, NYX, e.l.f., Revolution, Milani and Australis.

Long-wearing formulas and a setting spray are practical picks for a Gold Coast summer, when heat and humidity test any makeup. Our guide to [wearing sunscreen under makeup](/sunscreen-under-makeup/) has more tips for hot days.

## For the skincare fan

Skincare gift sets and minis are a low-risk way to try a new brand. We stock La Roche-Posay, CeraVe, Avène, Bioderma, QV, Neutrogena, Olay, The Ordinary, No7, Clinique and L’Oréal Paris, among others.

If the person has sensitive skin, choose a fragrance-free option. ASCIA lists perfumes among the common causes of allergic contact dermatitis, an itchy skin reaction. Suggest they try anything new on a small patch of skin first.

## For him

Men’s fragrance from Giorgio Armani, Hugo Boss, Calvin Klein, Jean Paul Gaultier, Versace and Rabanne is the classic choice. If you’re not sure of his taste, a grooming set from Gillette, NIVEA Men, L’Oréal Men Expert, Braun, Bulldog or American Crew is a safe bet.

For summer on the coast, add a broad-spectrum SPF 50+ face sunscreen. Cancer Council Queensland recommends sunscreen as one of five ways to protect your skin, alongside clothing, a hat, shade and sunglasses.

## A practical gift: an electric toothbrush

An electric toothbrush suits almost anyone, from a teenager to an older relative who finds brushing harder. A Cochrane review found powered toothbrushes reduce plaque and gum inflammation more than manual brushes. Whichever brush they use, the Australian Dental Association recommends brushing twice a day for two minutes.

Our dental range includes electric toothbrushes from Oral-B, and our [electric versus manual toothbrush guide](/electric-vs-manual-toothbrush/) compares the options.

## For teens

Celebrity fragrances from Ariana Grande and Billie Eilish are popular, along with affordable makeup from e.l.f., NYX and Revolution. A skincare starter set with a gentle cleanser, a moisturiser and an SPF 50+ sunscreen is a thoughtful gift for a teenager starting a routine.

## Stocking fillers

Small gifts that people actually use:

- lip balms and tinted lip oils
- hand creams
- nail polish
- sheet masks
- mini fragrances and body mists
- travel-size skincare
- an SPF 50+ lip balm or sunscreen stick for summer
