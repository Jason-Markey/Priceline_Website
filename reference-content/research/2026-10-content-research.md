## Compliance reminders

- No prescription (S4/S8) brand or drug promotion. Educational content about a *class* or *service* only.
- Sunscreens are therapeutic goods: never "waterproof", "sweatproof", "all-day", "sunblock"; quote label water-resistance only; SPF50+ only if tested ≥60; cite Cancer Council wording.
- Cosmetics: no "treats acne/eczema/rosacea" claims for cosmetics; "appearance of" wording stays cosmetic.
- Complementary medicines: claims must match ARTG-listed indication; "may help support" at most; add "Always read the label and follow the directions for use."
- No testimonials, no "dermatologist recommended", no health-professional endorsement of a specific product.
- Infant formula: no brand comparison/recommendation, no idealising; preparation safety and "talk to your child health nurse" only.
- Head lice: no "kills 100%" / "prevents lice".
