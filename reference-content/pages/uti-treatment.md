# UTI Treatment with a Pharmacist

[Home](/) / [Pharmacy Services](/pharmacy-services/) / UTI Treatment

A urinary tract infection (UTI) can make every trip to the bathroom miserable. In Queensland, trained pharmacists can assess and, if suitable, treat uncomplicated bladder infections in women, without a GP visit. At Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair in Broadbeach, you can talk to one of our trained pharmacists in our private consultation room.

## Who can use this service?

The Queensland pharmacy UTI service is for women aged 18 to 65 with symptoms of an uncomplicated bladder infection (cystitis), such as:

-   burning or stinging when you pass urine
-   needing to go more often, or urgently
-   discomfort low in your tummy

## What happens

1.  Your pharmacist asks about your symptoms, health history and any medicines you take, in private.
2.  If the service is suitable for you, the pharmacist can supply a treatment and explain how to take it.
3.  If it isn't suitable, we'll refer you to a GP. [Help Medical](/help-medical/) is inside our store.

Ask us about the consultation fee when you call. It isn't covered by Medicare.

## When you'll need to see a GP instead

Pharmacists can't treat UTIs through this service if you:

-   are pregnant, or might be
-   are under 18 or over 65
-   are male
-   have frequent or recurring UTIs
-   have kidney problems, a urinary catheter or a weakened immune system

## Get urgent medical help if you have

-   a fever, chills or shaking
-   pain in your back or side
-   blood in your urine
-   nausea or vomiting

These can be signs the infection has spread to your kidneys. See a GP the same day, or go to an emergency department. In an emergency, call 000.

## Simple things that can help

-   Drink plenty of water, especially in Gold Coast heat.
-   Don't hold on when you need to go.
-   Ask our pharmacists about products to ease discomfort while you wait for treatment to work.
