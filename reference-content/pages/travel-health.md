# Travel Health & Travel Vaccinations

[Home](/) / [Pharmacy Services](/pharmacy-services/) / Travel Health

## Start 6 to 8 weeks before you travel

Some vaccines need more than one dose, or take a couple of weeks to give full protection. If you can, talk to us 6 to 8 weeks before you leave. Leaving it late? Come in anyway. Some protection is better than none, and we can tell you what's still possible.

## Travel vaccines we offer

Depending on your destination and health, you may need protection against:

-   hepatitis A and hepatitis B
-   typhoid
-   cholera
-   Japanese encephalitis
-   polio
-   measles, mumps and rubella (if you're not fully vaccinated)
-   flu, and whooping cough boosters

Most travel vaccines aren't covered by the National Immunisation Program, so a private fee usually applies. You'll see current prices when you book online. Our pharmacists vaccinate people aged 5 and over.

## When you'll need a GP or travel clinic

Some destinations call for vaccines or medicines that need a prescription or a specialist travel consultation, such as yellow fever vaccination, rabies protection, or medicines to prevent malaria. If that's the case, we'll refer you to a GP at [Help Medical](/help-medical/), inside our store, or to a travel medicine clinic.

## Your travel health kit

We can help you put together a kit for your trip. Typical items include:

-   plasters, blister pads, antiseptic and a small bandage
-   pain and fever relief
-   oral rehydration solution for traveller's diarrhoea
-   insect repellent suitable for the region you're visiting
-   SPF 50+ sunscreen and after-sun gel
-   travel sickness remedies (ask us which suit you)
-   hand sanitiser

## Travelling with your regular medicines

-   Pack enough for the whole trip plus a few extra days, in your carry-on luggage, in the original packaging.
-   Carry a copy of your prescriptions and a letter from your doctor for any controlled or injectable medicines.
-   Check the rules of the country you're visiting, as some medicines that are legal in Australia are restricted overseas.
-   If you're taking PBS medicines overseas, there are limits on how much you can take. Ask us before you go.
