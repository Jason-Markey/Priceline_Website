## Order on Uber Eats

[**Order from Priceline on Uber Eats**](https://www.ubereats.com/au/brand/priceline)

We're the Priceline store delivering on Uber Eats on the Gold Coast, and we deliver right across the Gold Coast. Open the Uber Eats app, search for Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair, and choose from the Priceline range, including:

-   skincare, sunscreen and self-tan
-   makeup, fragrance, haircare and nail care
-   vitamins and supplements
-   first aid supplies
-   cold and flu, allergy and pain relief products that don't need a pharmacist's consultation
-   bath and body, baby care and household health essentials

Delivery times depend on where you are and how busy it is, and Uber Eats shows you an estimate before you order.

### What can't be delivered through Uber Eats

Prescription medicines can't be ordered through Uber Eats, and pharmacist-only medicines need a conversation with a pharmacist before you can buy them. For these, please visit us in store or call [(07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099).

## Prescription delivery

We don't offer a regular prescription delivery service. In an urgent situation, call us: if a team member is available, we may be able to arrange a delivery for a fee, but we can't guarantee it. The quickest option is usually to send your eScript ahead so it's ready to collect. [More about prescriptions](/prescriptions/).

## Click & Collect

Shop online at priceline.com.au, choose Priceline Pacific Fair as your store at checkout, and collect from us. Orders are usually ready within 15 minutes, and within 2 hours at the latest when everything is in stock. [See how Click & Collect works](/click-and-collect/).
