## Free skincare advice, no booking needed

Not sure where to start, or tired of products that don't suit you? Just come in and chat with one of our trained beauty advisors. We'll ask about your skin, your current routine and your budget, then recommend a few products that will make a real difference. There's no charge and no need to book.

## Help for common skin concerns

### Sensitive or reactive skin

Gentle cleansers, fragrance-free moisturisers and barrier-repair products for skin that stings, flushes or reacts easily.

### Dry and dehydrated skin

Hydrating serums and richer creams, plus tips for air-conditioned offices and cooler winter mornings.

### Breakout-prone and oily skin

Lightweight, non-comedogenic products that work in humid weather. If breakouts are persistent, painful or scarring, our pharmacists can talk you through options and refer you to a GP or dermatologist where needed.

### Ageing and sun-damaged skin

Daily sun protection, antioxidant serums and retinoid-style products, with advice on introducing them slowly.

### Pigmentation and uneven tone

Products and sun-protection habits to help with an uneven skin tone. See a doctor about any spot that's new, changing or different from the others.
