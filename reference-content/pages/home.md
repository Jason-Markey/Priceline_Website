# Your local pharmacy at Pacific Fair, Broadbeach.  
Your well-being is our priority.

Welcome to **Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair**, a locally owned community pharmacy on the ground floor of Pacific Fair Shopping Centre in Broadbeach. Whether you’re filling a script, getting a flu shot on your lunch break, or looking for skincare that suits the Gold Coast climate, our pharmacists and beauty advisors take the time to get it right for you.

**Why locals choose us**

-   **Pharmacists who make time for you**: ask us anything, no appointment needed.
-   **Fast, accurate dispensing** with our robotic dispensary. [Send your script ahead](/prescriptions/).
-   **GPs in store** at [Help Medical](/help-medical/), a bulk-billing clinic for eligible patients.
-   **Walk-in [vaccinations](/vaccination-information/)**, with free government-funded vaccines for eligible people and no service fee.
-   **Beauty advice you can trust** for [skincare](/skincare-tanning/), makeup and [fragrance](/beauty-fragrance/).
-   **Delivered to your door** anywhere on the Gold Coast through [Uber Eats](/delivery/).
-   **Open 7 days**, and until 9pm on Thursdays.
