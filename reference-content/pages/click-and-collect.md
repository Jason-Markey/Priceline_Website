# Click & Collect at Pacific Fair

[Home](/) / [Pharmacy Services](/pharmacy-services/) / Click & Collect

Shop the full Priceline range online, then pick up your order from Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair in Broadbeach. Orders are usually ready within 15 minutes.

[Shop priceline.com.au](https://www.priceline.com.au) [Hours & directions](/visit-us/)

## How it works

1.  **Shop online.** Go to [priceline.com.au](https://www.priceline.com.au) and add your items to your cart.
2.  **Choose Pacific Fair.** At checkout, select Click & Collect and choose Priceline Pacific Fair as your store.
3.  **Wait for your message.** We'll let you know when your order is ready. That's usually within 15 minutes, and within 2 hours at the latest when everything is in stock.
4.  **Collect.** Show your order confirmation at the counter and we'll hand it over.

## Good to know

-   Click & Collect is for general products. Prescription medicines can't be ordered this way; see [prescriptions](/prescriptions/) to send your eScript ahead.
-   There's no delivery fee, because you're collecting from us.

## Prefer delivery?

We deliver across the Gold Coast through Uber Eats. See our [delivery options](/delivery/).
