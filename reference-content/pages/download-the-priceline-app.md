# Download the Priceline Pharmacy App

[Home](/) / [Pharmacy Services](/pharmacy-services/) / Priceline App

Keep your scripts, reminders and rewards in one place with the Priceline Pharmacy app, then collect from us at Pacific Fair, Broadbeach.

[Download for iPhone](https://itunes.apple.com/au/app/priceline-pharmacy-app/id1440942154?mt=8) [Download for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=au.com.priceline.pricelineapp)

## With the app you can

-   order your prescription medicines ahead from Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair, so they're ready when you arrive
-   add eScripts by pasting the token link or scanning the QR code
-   get reminders when it's time to take a medicine or order a repeat
-   keep track of your scripts
-   access your Sister Club account and rewards

[Download for iPhone](https://itunes.apple.com/au/app/priceline-pharmacy-app/id1440942154?mt=8) · [Download for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=au.com.priceline.pricelineapp)

## Set Pacific Fair as your pharmacy

When you set up the app, choose Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair (Shop 81, Pacific Fair Shopping Centre, Broadbeach) as your preferred store so your orders come to us.
