# Book a Contraception Consultation

[Home](/) / [Pharmacy Services](/pharmacy-services/) / Book a Contraception Consultation

Our trained pharmacists at Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair can help women aged 16 and over start, change or continue their contraception, including the pill, the vaginal ring and the contraceptive injection, without first seeing a GP.

[Call to book: (07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099)

Consultations take about 10 to 20 minutes and cost $35. Your contraception is charged separately. Trained pharmacists are usually available Monday to Friday, so please call first to book.

**Call to book:** [(07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099)
