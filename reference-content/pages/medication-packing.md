# Medication Packing (Webster Packs) at Pacific Fair

[Home](/) / [Pharmacy Services](/pharmacy-services/) / Medication Packing

Keeping track of several medicines is hard, especially when doses change or you're caring for someone else. Our medication packing service at Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair in Broadbeach sorts your medicines into weekly blister packs, often called Webster packs, with each dose clearly labelled by day and time.

[Call (07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099) [Hours & directions](/visit-us/)

## What does it cost?

Medication packing is **free for concession and health care card holders**, and $5 a week for everyone else.

## How it works

1.  **Talk to us.** Bring in your current medicines and prescriptions, or ask your doctor to send scripts to us.
2.  **We set up your profile.** A pharmacist checks every medicine, dose and timing, and flags anything that needs a question to your doctor.
3.  **We pack your medicines.** Each week's medicines are sealed into a blister pack labelled with the day and time of each dose.
4.  **You collect.** Pick up your packs each week, or have a family member or carer collect for you.

## Who it helps

-   People taking several medicines at different times of day
-   Anyone who sometimes forgets whether they've taken a dose
-   Older people living independently
-   Family members and carers managing medicines for someone else
-   Anyone whose medicines change often after hospital visits or specialist appointments

## Caring for a parent or partner?

Blister packs make it easy to see at a glance whether today's doses have been taken, which gives you peace of mind if you don't live with the person you care for. Let us know who else can collect packs or talk to us about their medicines, and we'll note it on their profile.

## When a medicine changes

If a doctor changes a dose or adds a new medicine, bring or send us the new script as soon as possible. We'll update the packs and let you know when the new ones are ready. Please don't try to push tablets out or add them yourself.
