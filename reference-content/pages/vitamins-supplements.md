## Why ask a pharmacist first?

-   **Interactions.** Some supplements can affect how prescription medicines work, including blood thinners, some antidepressants and the contraceptive pill.
-   **Doubling up.** It's easy to take the same ingredient in two products without realising.
-   **Whether you need it at all.** Sometimes a blood test through your GP is the better first step, for example if you think you're low in iron or vitamin D.
-   **Pregnancy and breastfeeding.** We can help you choose supplements that are appropriate at each stage.

## A note on expectations

Supplements can help fill gaps in your diet, but they don't replace a balanced diet, and they aren't a substitute for medical care. If you have ongoing symptoms such as tiredness, see your GP. The doctors at [Help Medical](/help-medical/) are located inside our store.
