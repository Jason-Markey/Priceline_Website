## Makeup

From everyday essentials to the latest launches, our advisors can help you match foundation shades, find long-wearing formulas for hot days, and build a simple kit that works. ## Fragrance

Choosing a fragrance is personal. Try before you buy, and give a scent a few hours on your skin before deciding, because it changes as it settles. Our team can suggest options based on what you already love, and help you choose a gift when you're not sure of someone's taste.

Our fragrance range includes YSL, Giorgio Armani, Prada, Lancôme, Estée Lauder, Versace, Dolce & Gabbana, Marc Jacobs, Jimmy Choo, Rabanne, Jean Paul Gaultier, Hugo Boss, Calvin Klein, Ariana Grande and Billie Eilish, with options for every budget, from body mists and smaller celebrity fragrances to full-size designer perfumes.

Heat and humidity make fragrance project more, so a lighter concentration or a fresher scent often works best for Gold Coast days.
