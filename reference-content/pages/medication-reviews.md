# Medication Reviews at Pacific Fair

[Home](/) / [Pharmacy Services](/pharmacy-services/) / Medication Reviews

If you take several medicines, it's worth having someone look at the whole picture now and then. A medication review is a one-on-one conversation with a pharmacist at Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair in Broadbeach. We go through everything you take, including vitamins and over-the-counter products, to make sure it's all working well together.

[Call (07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099) [Hours & directions](/visit-us/)

## Free for Medicare card holders

Medication reviews are free if you have a Medicare card. There's no need to book: just ask one of our pharmacists.

## Who might benefit

-   People taking five or more medicines
-   Anyone recently home from hospital, or whose medicines have changed
-   People experiencing side effects, or unsure whether a medicine is still needed
-   Anyone finding it hard to remember when to take their medicines
-   Carers managing medicines for someone else

## What happens

1.  Bring all your medicines in a bag, including vitamins, supplements, creams, inhalers and eye drops.
2.  Your pharmacist goes through each one with you: what it's for, how you take it, and how it's going.
3.  We check for interactions, doubling up and side effects.
4.  You leave with an up-to-date medicines list, and we'll let your doctor know if anything needs attention.
