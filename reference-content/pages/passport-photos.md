-   No appointment needed
-   Australian and overseas passport and visa photos
-   Ages 5 and above
-   Printed and digital copies of your photos

## Tips for a photo that’s accepted first time

**Neutral expression**, with your mouth closed and eyes open.  
**No glasses**: Australian passports don’t accept photos with glasses.  
**Face uncovered**: hair away from your eyes and no hats. Religious head coverings are fine if your full face is visible.  
**Plain clothing** that contrasts with a light background.  
**Recent**: photos must be taken within the last six months.

For full requirements, see the [Australian Passport Office](https://www.passports.gov.au/). Applying for another country’s passport or visa? Tell us which one and we’ll set up the right format.
