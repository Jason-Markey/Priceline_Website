## Signs it may be time for a hearing test

-   You often ask people to repeat themselves, especially in noisy places.
-   Family say the TV is too loud.
-   You have ringing or buzzing in your ears.
-   One ear feels blocked or muffled, which can also be caused by wax.

See a GP urgently if you have sudden hearing loss in one ear, or ear pain with fever or discharge. The GPs at [Help Medical](/help-medical/) are inside our store.
