# Common Illnesses A–Z

[Home](/) / Common Illnesses A–Z

Plain-English guides to common health problems, written for Australians by the team at Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair in Broadbeach and reviewed by our pharmacist, Jason Markey (BPharmSci, MPharm). Each guide explains the symptoms, what you can do at home, how a pharmacist can help, and when to see a GP. For personal advice, just ask us in store.

-   [**Acne**](/acne-treatment-pharmacy/): what causes breakouts, and which pharmacy options can help.
-   [**Athlete's foot**](/athletes-foot-treatment/): how to clear it, and how to stop it coming back in Gold Coast humidity.
-   **Cold sores**: tingling, blisters and sun triggers. Our pharmacists can suggest treatments to use at the first tingle.
-   [**Common cold**](/common-cold-remedies/): myths, facts, and what really helps you feel better.
-   [**Constipation**](/constipation-relief/): simple changes and pharmacy options, and when to see a GP.

-   [**Dry skin**](/dry-skin-remedies/): a pharmacist's checklist for itchy, dry skin.

## E–H

-   **Eczema**: keeping skin moisturised and avoiding triggers such as heat, sweat and chlorine. Ask our pharmacists about flare-up options.
-   [**Haemorrhoids**](/haemorrhoids-treatment/): myths, facts and home care.

-   [**Headaches**](/headache-relief/): common types, what helps, and the warning signs to act on.
-   [**Heartburn and reflux (GORD)**](/heartburn-relief/): what actually helps, and when to see a GP.

## S–Z

-   [**Scabies**](/scabies-treatment-australia/): treatment, and how to stop it spreading at home.
-   [**Sprains and strains**](/sprain-vs-strain/): the difference, and first aid that helps.

-   **Travel sickness**: tips for theme park rides, boats and road trips. Ask us which travel sickness remedies suit you and your children.

## When to get help

These guides are general information. If you're unsure, symptoms are severe, or they aren't improving, talk to our pharmacists or see a GP. [Help Medical](/help-medical/) is inside our store. In an emergency, call 000.
