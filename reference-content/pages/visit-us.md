## Parking

Pacific Fair offers **4 hours of free parking** each day, with ticketless entry and exit that reads your number plate. Free all-day parking is available with an accessible parking permit.

-   **Car park below us:** from the Gold Coast Highway, cross the bridge into Pacific Fair and turn right into the car park beneath the north-eastern side of the centre.
-   **Undercover rooftop parking:** head to the north-eastern side of the centre and park on the undercover rooftop level above us.

For car park maps, rates beyond 4 hours and accessible parking, see [Pacific Fair's parking page](https://www.pacificfair.com.au/centre-info/getting-here-parking1/car-parking).

## Light rail and buses

Take the G:link light rail to **Broadbeach South** station, on the Gold Coast Highway right out the front of Pacific Fair. It's a short walk into the centre, and we're close to the Gold Coast Highway side. Many bus routes also stop at Broadbeach South and at the Pacific Fair bus interchange on Hooker Boulevard. Plan your trip with [Translink](https://jp.translink.com.au/).

## Finding us inside Pacific Fair

### The store

You'll find us on the ground floor, next to Proud Smile on our right, with the outdoor entrance from the river on our left.

### The prescription counter

Once you're inside, the prescription counter is on the left, at the very back of the store.

### Help Medical

[Help Medical](/help-medical/) is inside our store, on the right-hand side as you walk in.
