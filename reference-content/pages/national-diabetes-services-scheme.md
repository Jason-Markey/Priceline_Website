# Diabetes Supplies & the NDSS at Pacific Fair

[Home](/) / [Pharmacy Services](/pharmacy-services/) / Diabetes & NDSS

Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair in Broadbeach is an access point for the National Diabetes Services Scheme (NDSS). If you're registered, you can collect subsidised diabetes supplies from us and get advice from our pharmacists about managing your diabetes day to day.

[Call (07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099) [Hours & directions](/visit-us/)

## What is the NDSS?

The NDSS is an Australian Government initiative, administered by Diabetes Australia, that helps people with diabetes access subsidised products and support. You can learn more at [ndss.com.au](https://www.ndss.com.au/).

## How to register

Registration needs a form signed by your doctor or credentialled diabetes educator. The GPs at [Help Medical](/help-medical/), inside our store, can help. Once you're registered, just show your NDSS card or number when you collect supplies.

## Supplies you can collect

-   blood glucose test strips
-   urine and blood ketone test strips
-   pen needles and syringes
-   insulin pump consumables
-   continuous and flash glucose monitoring products, for eligible people

We stock products for all the major meter and monitoring brands, including Accu-Chek, FreeStyle, Contour and CareSens. If we don't have your product on the shelf, we can order it in.

Medicines for diabetes, such as tablets and insulin, are prescription medicines. See [prescriptions](/prescriptions/).

## Support from our pharmacists

-   setting up and using a new meter
-   storing insulin safely, including in Gold Coast summer heat
-   sick-day advice and what to do if your levels are out of range
-   checking interactions with other medicines
-   safe disposal of sharps

## Know your risk

Not diagnosed but worried about type 2 diabetes? Try a free AUSDRISK risk check at our [Health Station](/health-station/).
