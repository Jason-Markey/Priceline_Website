# Contraception Consultations with a Pharmacist

[Home](/) / [Pharmacy Services](/pharmacy-services/) / Contraception

In Queensland, specially trained pharmacists can help women aged 16 and over start, change or continue their contraception without first seeing a GP. At Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair in Broadbeach, you can have this consultation in our private consultation room.

**Book a consultation:** call [(07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099). We have several trained pharmacists, usually available Monday to Friday, but it's best to call first, because consultations take a little longer.

## What we can prescribe

If it's suitable for you, our trained pharmacists can prescribe:

-   the combined oral contraceptive pill
-   the progestogen-only pill (the "mini-pill")
-   the vaginal ring
-   the contraceptive injection (ask us how the injection is given)

For longer-acting options, such as an implant or IUD, we'll refer you to a GP. The GPs at [Help Medical](/help-medical/), inside our store, offer IUD insertion.

## Who can use this service?

The Queensland service is for women aged 16 and over who want to start, restart, change or continue their contraception. It became a permanent service on 1 July 2025. If the method you're interested in isn't the safest choice for you, we'll explain why and refer you to a GP.

## What happens in the consultation

A consultation usually takes 10 to 20 minutes. Your pharmacist will:

-   go through your health and medical history, and any medicines you take;
-   check your blood pressure;
-   check your height and weight;
-   talk about your previous experience with contraception and what matters most to you;
-   explain your options, how to use your contraception correctly, and what to do if you miss a dose.

If it's appropriate, your pharmacist can then write your prescription and dispense it for you. With your permission, we'll send a summary to your regular GP.

## What does it cost?

The consultation costs $35. Your contraception is charged separately, and many types are listed on the PBS. There's no Medicare rebate for pharmacist consultations.

## When we'll refer you to a GP

Sometimes a GP is the better next step. For example, if something in your health history means a method may not be safe for you, if your blood pressure reading is high, or if you'd like an implant or IUD.

## Your privacy

Consultations happen in a private room, and your information is handled confidentially. You're welcome to bring a support person.

## Common questions

### Do I need a script from my doctor?

No. If you're eligible, the pharmacist can provide the prescription as part of the consultation.

### Can I get emergency contraception?

Emergency contraception is available from our pharmacists without an appointment. Ask at the counter and we'll talk with you privately.

### I'm already on the pill and my script has run out. Can you help?

Yes. This service can be used to continue contraception you're already using, if you're eligible.
