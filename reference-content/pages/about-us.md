# About Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair

[Home](/) / About Us

Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair is a locally owned community pharmacy on the ground floor of Pacific Fair Shopping Centre in Broadbeach. Being locally owned means we can shape our services around the people we see every day: Gold Coast locals, families, workers and the many visitors who pass through Pacific Fair.

[Call (07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099) [Hours & directions](/visit-us/)

## What we care about

-   **Time for you.** We'd rather spend a few extra minutes getting to the bottom of a problem than rush you through. If we can't help, we'll refer you, often to the GPs at [Help Medical](/help-medical/), right inside our store.
-   **Expert advice.** Our pharmacists and beauty advisors are here to answer questions about medicines, wellbeing, skincare and beauty.
-   **Fair prices.** Competitive pricing without cutting corners on care.
-   **Better technology, more time with you.** Our robotic dispensary picks medicines in seconds, and electronic shelf labels keep prices accurate. Both free up our team to spend more time with customers. You can also order everyday items for delivery across the Gold Coast through [Uber Eats](/delivery/).

## Meet the team

### Jason Markey, Owner and Pharmacist (BPharmSci, MPharm)

Jason bought Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair in December 2023, bringing his career full circle: this is the store where he first trained. He completed his Master of Pharmacy at Griffith University, finished his internship in 2015, and spent five years with Priceline before working as a locum pharmacist across North Queensland. That experience of seeing what works, and what doesn't, in many different pharmacies shapes how the store runs today. Jason also reviews the health articles on our website.

### Chelsea Morey, Dispensary Manager

Chelsea has worked in pharmacies across the Gold Coast and brings strong organisational skills to our dispensary, making sure every prescription is handled accurately and efficiently.

### Ben Wilson, Store Manager

Ben has been part of Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair since it opened in 2009. With deep experience in retail and management, and a keen interest in technology, he keeps the store running smoothly and has led many of the improvements customers notice.
