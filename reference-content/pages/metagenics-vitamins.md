## About Metagenics

Metagenics is a practitioner range, which means its products are usually recommended by a health professional after talking about your health and goals. The range covers general wellbeing, digestive health, energy, stress, sleep, sport and more.

## Can't see what you need? We'll order it in

We keep a selection of Metagenics products on the shelf, and we can special-order anything else from their range. We'll give you a call when it arrives. Delivery time depends on postage, so order a little before you run out.

## Why buy from a pharmacy?

-   **Checked against your medicines.** Our pharmacists can check whether a supplement is suitable alongside any prescription or over-the-counter medicines you take.
-   **Honest advice.** If a supplement isn't likely to help, or a GP visit is the better first step, we'll tell you.
