# Prescriptions at Pacific Fair, Broadbeach

[Home](/) / [Pharmacy Services](/pharmacy-services/) / Prescriptions

Filling a script at Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair is quick and simple. Bring in a paper script, show us an eScript on your phone, or send it ahead through the app so it's ready when you arrive. Our pharmacists will take the time to answer your questions. We're on the ground floor of Pacific Fair Shopping Centre in Broadbeach, open 7 days and until 9pm on Thursdays.

[Call (07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099) [Hours & directions](/visit-us/)

## Send your eScript ahead

The easiest way to skip the wait is to send your eScript to us before you arrive, using the MedAdvisor app or the [Priceline Pharmacy app](/download-the-priceline-app/):

1.  Open the app (or log in to MedAdvisor in your browser).
2.  Add your eScript: paste the token link, scan the QR code, or use Snap n Send.
3.  Choose **Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair**, Shop 81, Pacific Fair Shopping Centre, Broadbeach QLD 4218.
4.  Submit your order and we'll let you know when it's ready to collect.

## Other ways to fill your prescription

### Show us your eScript in store

Come to the prescription counter at the back of the store and show us the QR code in your SMS or email.

### Paper scripts

Hand your script to us at the prescription counter. If you'd rather not wait, do some shopping and collect it on your way out.

### Active Script List

An Active Script List keeps all your eScripts in one place, so you don't need to keep track of individual tokens. Ask us and we can register you in store. Once you're set up, with your consent we can find your current scripts whenever you visit.

### Scripts on file

We can keep your repeat prescriptions on file, so you don't need to bring the paperwork each time.

### Hospital and private prescriptions

We dispense hospital discharge scripts and private prescriptions as well as scripts from your GP.

## Fast, accurate dispensing

Our dispensary uses a robotic system that stores and picks medicines automatically. It finds the right medicine in seconds, which frees our pharmacists to spend more time checking your prescription and talking with you, not searching shelves.

## Hard-to-find medicines

We work with several wholesalers and can order in most medicines, including specialised and less common ones. If a medicine is short nationally, we'll let you know your options and speak to your doctor if needed. Call us on [(07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099) or email [pharmacy@pricelinepf.com.au](mailto:pharmacy@pricelinepf.com.au) to check availability.

## Never run out

-   **[Priceline Pharmacy app](/download-the-priceline-app/).** Order your scripts and get reminders when repeats are due.
-   **[Medication packing](/medication-packing/).** Your medicines organised by day and time in weekly blister packs.
-   **[Medication reviews](/medication-reviews/).** A free one-on-one check if you take several medicines.

## Need a new script?

The GPs at [Help Medical](/help-medical/) are located inside our store, so you can see a doctor and fill your prescription in one visit.

## Can you deliver my prescription?

We don't offer a regular prescription delivery service, and prescription medicines can't be ordered through Uber Eats. In an urgent situation, call us: if a team member is available, we may be able to arrange a delivery for a fee, but we can't guarantee it. For everyday health and beauty products, see our [delivery options](/delivery/).

## Questions about your medicine?

Ask us anything: how to take it, side effects, or whether it's safe with your other medicines, vitamins or supplements. There's no appointment needed.
