# Book an Appointment

[Home](/) / Appointments

Most of our services don't need an appointment, so you can just walk in. If you'd prefer a set time, here's how to book each service at Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair in Broadbeach.

[Book online](https://healthengine.com.au/pharmacy/qld/broadbeach/priceline-pharmacy-pacific-fair/s54055) [Call (07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099)

-   **[Vaccinations](/vaccination-information/):** walk in, or [book online](https://healthengine.com.au/pharmacy/qld/broadbeach/priceline-pharmacy-pacific-fair/s54055).
-   **Pharmacist consultations and health checks:** [book online](https://healthengine.com.au/pharmacy/qld/broadbeach/priceline-pharmacy-pacific-fair/s54055), or ask at the counter.
-   **[Contraception consultations](/hormonal-contraceptive-pill/):** call [(07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099) to book.
-   **[UTI treatment](/uti-treatment/):** call [(07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099) to check a trained pharmacist is on.
-   **[GP at Help Medical](/help-medical/):** book at [helpmedical.au](https://helpmedical.au/) or call [(07) 5619 3818](tel:+61756193818).
-   **[Medication reviews](/medication-reviews/):** just ask a pharmacist.
-   **[Ear piercing](/ear-piercings/):** walk in.
-   **[Passport photos](/passport-photos/):** walk in, no booking needed.
-   **[Blood pressure and health checks](/health-station/):** walk in.
-   **[Hearing tests and ear wax removal](/audiology-services/):** see Simply Hearing at Pacific Fair.

## Not sure what you need?

Ask a pharmacist. There's no appointment needed. Call [(07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099) or come and see us.
