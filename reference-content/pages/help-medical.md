# Help Medical: GPs Inside Priceline Pacific Fair

[Home](/) / [Pharmacy Services](/pharmacy-services/) / Help Medical

Need to see a doctor? Help Medical is a general practice located right inside Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair in Broadbeach. See a GP, then have your prescription filled at our dispensary a few steps away, all in one visit.

[Book online](https://helpmedical.au/) [Call (07) 5619 3818](tel:+61756193818)

![Help Medical reception desk and waiting area inside Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair](https://pricelinepacificfair.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Help_Medical_10052022-11-2048x1365-1-1024x683.jpg)

## Bulk billing

Help Medical is a bulk-billing clinic for eligible patients with a Medicare card. Conditions apply, for example on weekends and public holidays, so please check when you book. Patients without Medicare pay a private fee.

## Walk-ins and appointments

Help Medical welcomes walk-ins and does its best to fit you in, subject to doctor availability. On busy days appointments fill quickly, so booking ahead is the surest way to be seen. New patients and Gold Coast visitors are welcome.

## Opening hours

-   **Monday to Friday:** 8:00am – 5:00pm
-   **Saturday:** 9:00am – 4:00pm
-   **Sunday:** 9:30am – 4:00pm

## What the GPs can help with

Help Medical offers the full range of general practice care, including:

-   check-ups, health assessments and same-day illness
-   prescriptions, repeat scripts and referrals
-   women's health, contraception and IUD insertion
-   men's health
-   antenatal and postnatal care
-   children's and adolescent health
-   skin checks and minor skin procedures
-   vaccinations and travel health
-   mental health care
-   chronic disease management
-   work medicals and WorkCover

## Doctor and pharmacist, working together

Having GPs and pharmacists under one roof means your script can go straight to our dispensary, and our pharmacists can refer you to a doctor quickly if you need one. If you have questions about a new medicine after your appointment, just ask at the counter.
