# Pharmacy Services at Pacific Fair

[Home](/) / Pharmacy Services

Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair offers far more than prescriptions. Here's everything our pharmacists and team can help with at our store on the ground floor of Pacific Fair Shopping Centre, Broadbeach. Most services don't need an appointment. Just walk in.

[Call (07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099) [Book an appointment](/appointments/)

## Prescriptions and medicines

-   **[Prescriptions and eScripts](/prescriptions/).** Paper scripts, eScripts, Active Script Lists, hospital scripts and hard-to-find medicines ordered in.
-   **[Medication packing (Webster packs)](/medication-packing/).** Your medicines sorted by day and time in easy blister packs.
-   **[Medication reviews](/medication-reviews/).** A free one-on-one check of all your medicines with a pharmacist, for Medicare card holders.
-   **[Priceline Pharmacy app](/download-the-priceline-app/).** Order scripts and get reminders on your phone.
-   **[Delivery and Click & Collect](/delivery/).** Everyday health and beauty products delivered across the Gold Coast, or order online and collect.

## Vaccinations and health checks

-   **[Vaccinations](/vaccination-information/).** Flu, whooping cough, shingles, COVID-19 and more. Walk-ins welcome.
-   **[Travel health](/travel-health/).** Travel vaccines and advice before you fly.
-   **[Health Station](/health-station/).** Free blood pressure, weight, BMI and type 2 diabetes risk checks.
-   **[Diabetes and the NDSS](/national-diabetes-services-scheme/).** Diabetes supplies and support.
-   **[Weight management support](/weight-loss-counselling-and-advice/).** Free one-on-one pharmacist advice.

## Women's health

-   **[Contraception consultations](/hormonal-contraceptive-pill/).** Start, change or continue your contraception with a trained pharmacist.
-   **[UTI treatment](/uti-treatment/).** Assessment and treatment for uncomplicated urinary tract infections, for eligible women.
-   **Emergency contraception.** Available from our pharmacists without an appointment.
-   **[More women's health services](/womens-health/).**

## Everyday services

-   **[Passport and visa photos](/passport-photos/).** Taken while you wait, no appointment needed.
-   **[Ear piercing](/ear-piercings/).** Walk-ins welcome.
-   **[Skincare and self-tan advice](/skincare-tanning/).** From our beauty advisors.
-   **[Beauty and fragrance](/beauty-fragrance/).** Makeup, fragrance, dental care and men's grooming.
-   **[Vitamins and supplements](/vitamins-supplements/).** Including practitioner ranges, with pharmacist advice.
-   **Return unwanted medicines.** Bring back expired or unwanted medicines for free, safe disposal.

## In-store partner

-   **[Help Medical](/help-medical/).** A bulk-billing GP clinic located inside our store.

Looking for a hearing test or ear wax removal? See [hearing services near us at Pacific Fair](/audiology-services/).
