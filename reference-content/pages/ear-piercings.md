# Ear Piercing at Pacific Fair

[Home](/) / [Pharmacy Services](/pharmacy-services/) / Ear Piercing

Getting your ears pierced should be quick, clean and relaxed. At Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair in Broadbeach, our trained team uses a hygienic, single-use system in a pharmacy setting, and sends you home with aftercare advice. Walk-ins are welcome, with no booking needed.

## At a glance

-   **Price:** $65, including the piercing, your earrings, antiseptic aftercare spray and a consultation with our team.
-   **Who:** ages 8 and over. Under-16s need a parent or guardian with them to give consent.
-   **Booking:** just walk in during opening hours.
-   **Re-piercing:** available for piercings that have closed or were placed incorrectly.

## Studs and earrings

-   Studs suitable for sensitive ears, made from lead-free materials
-   Gold-plated and sterling silver earrings
-   Clear retainer studs for work or school

Earrings are also sold separately, from $12.95.

## What to expect

1.  We talk you through the process and confirm consent (a parent or guardian gives consent for under-16s).
2.  You choose your studs.
3.  We clean the area and mark the position, and you check it in the mirror.
4.  The piercing itself takes seconds.
5.  We go through aftercare with you before you leave.

## Aftercare

-   Leave your starter studs in for at least 6 weeks for earlobes, and around 12 weeks for cartilage piercings. Piercings keep healing on the inside for months after that.
-   Clean the piercing twice a day with the aftercare spray or salt water, without taking the studs out. Wash your hands first.
-   Don't use alcohol or tea tree oil on a new piercing.
-   Avoid swimming in pools, the ocean and spas until it has healed, which is worth planning around in a Gold Coast summer.
-   Keep hair products, make-up and perfume away from the area.

See us or your GP if the area becomes increasingly red, hot, swollen or painful, or if there's pus or a fever.
