# Health Station: Free Blood Pressure & Health Checks

[Home](/) / [Pharmacy Services](/pharmacy-services/) / Health Station

Knowing your numbers is one of the simplest ways to look after your health. At the Health Station inside Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair in Broadbeach, you can check your blood pressure and other key measures for free, with a pharmacist nearby to explain what they mean.

[Call (07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099) [Hours & directions](/visit-us/)

## What we can check

### Blood pressure

High blood pressure usually has no symptoms, which is why regular checks matter. It's a major risk factor for heart attack, stroke and kidney disease.

### Height, weight and BMI

A quick way to track changes over time. BMI is a rough guide only and doesn't account for things like muscle mass.

### AUSDRISK: type 2 diabetes risk

A short questionnaire that estimates your risk of developing type 2 diabetes in the next five years.

## How it works

-   It's free, and there's no appointment needed. Just come in during opening hours.
-   We can email your results to you, so you can track them or share them with your doctor.
-   Check in each time you fill your scripts, and you'll see how your health is changing over time.
-   If a result is outside the healthy range, our pharmacists can talk it through and refer you to a GP. [Help Medical](/help-medical/) is inside our store.

## What do blood pressure numbers mean?

A reading has two numbers, for example 120/80. The top number is the pressure when your heart beats; the bottom number is the pressure between beats. A single high reading doesn't mean you have high blood pressure, but it's a sign to check again and talk to your doctor.

## When to see a doctor straight away

If you have a very high reading along with chest pain, shortness of breath, severe headache, weakness, or changes in vision or speech, call 000.
