# Weight Management Support at Pacific Fair

[Home](/) / [Pharmacy Services](/pharmacy-services/) / Weight Management

Changing your weight is rarely just about willpower. Sleep, stress, medicines, health conditions and busy routines all play a part. Our pharmacists at Priceline Pharmacy Pacific Fair in Broadbeach offer free, one-on-one weight management support that fits around your life, including help if your doctor has prescribed a weight-loss medicine.

[Call (07) 5592 2099](tel:+61755922099) [Hours & directions](/visit-us/)

## How our pharmacists can help

-   **A private conversation.** We talk through your goals, your current medicines and anything that has made weight management harder in the past.
-   **Free health checks.** We can record your weight, BMI and blood pressure at our in-store [Health Station](/health-station/) and email you the results, so you can see your progress over time.
-   **Practical advice.** We cover eating patterns, activity you can realistically keep up, sleep, and staying hydrated in the Gold Coast heat.
-   **A local dietitian.** We work with a local dietitian and can put you in touch if you'd like tailored nutrition advice.

## If you've been prescribed a weight-loss medicine

Starting a new medicine can raise a lot of questions. Our pharmacists can help you:

-   understand how and when to take or use your medicine, including injection technique where relevant;
-   know which side effects are common, how to manage them, and when to contact your doctor;
-   store your medicine correctly, including in hot weather and when travelling;
-   check for interactions with your other medicines, vitamins and supplements;
-   plan ahead for repeat prescriptions, and let you know early if supply of your medicine is affected.

Only your doctor can decide whether a prescription weight-loss medicine is right for you. If you'd like to talk to a GP, [Help Medical](/help-medical/) is located inside our store.

## Who is this service for?

Anyone who wants support managing their weight, whether you're just getting started, restarting after a break, or already under the care of a doctor or dietitian. We'll work alongside your other health professionals, not replace them.

## What does it cost?

Nothing. Our pharmacists' support and advice is free, and you don't need to book. Just ask at the counter.

## When to see your GP

See your doctor if you have unexplained weight loss or gain, if you have a health condition such as diabetes or heart disease, or if you're pregnant or planning a pregnancy. Also see your doctor if you're taking a weight-loss medicine and have severe or persistent stomach pain, vomiting or other side effects that worry you. In an emergency, call 000.
