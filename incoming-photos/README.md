# Incoming photos

Drop new store photos here (JPG or PNG, ideally 2000 pixels wide or more), then tell Claude.

Nothing in this folder goes on the website by itself. Claude checks each photo (no customers' faces, no
prescription-only medicines, no medicine brands front and centre on health pages), resizes it, moves it into
`src/assets/images/` and sends you a before-and-after to approve.

Upload link (opens GitHub's upload page for this folder):
https://github.com/Jason-Markey/Priceline_Website/upload/claude/youthful-turing-bm5rj2/incoming-photos
